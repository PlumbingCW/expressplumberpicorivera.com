Which Pico this page means

Pico Rivera takes its name from two older communities, Pico and Rivera. Pico was named for Pío Pico, the last Mexican governor of California and an early landowner here. The Pico name still marks places across the city, including Pico Park at 9528 Beverly Blvd and Pio Pico Park at 4211 Columbia Ave, both listed by the City of Pico Rivera.

That is a different place from Pico Boulevard or the Pico-Union area of Los Angeles. If you are in the City of Pico Rivera, you are on the right page.

Boundaries for the old Pico community are not documented, and sources disagree on whether it lay north or south of Rivera. So this page works from a named anchor, Pico Park and Beverly Blvd, instead of a drawn border.

Owning and renting in Pico Rivera

The city's housing element reports that 67.3 percent of households own their home and 32.7 percent rent. Among owner-occupied homes, 93.4 percent are single-family detached houses. Those are citywide numbers from 2018 survey data.

Why it matters for a plumbing call: owners decide on repairs themselves, while renters usually need to tell the landlord first. Say which you are when you call. It helps to know who can approve a visit and who pays.

Before you call about a drain or heater problem

A few quick checks help you describe the problem.

  • ✓For a slow drain, note whether one fixture or several are affected.
