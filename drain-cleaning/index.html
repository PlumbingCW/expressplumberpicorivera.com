Gurgling, slow, or backing up: read the symptom

Most drain calls start with a small sign. A sink that takes a minute to empty, a tub that leaves a ring of grey water, or a gurgle from a different fixture when you run the washer. Each one points to a different spot in the line.

One slow fixture usually means a clog in that fixture's own branch. Two or three slow fixtures at once point to a shared line further down. Water coming up in the tub when you flush is the one to treat as urgent, because the blockage is in a line that serves the whole house.

Drain backup tonight? Do this while you wait

Water rising from a drain can spread into flooring fast. These steps limit the damage until the technician arrives.

  1. 1

    Stop all water use

    No flushing, no laundry, no dishwasher. Every gallon you send down has nowhere to go and may come back out of the lowest drain.

  2. 2

    Lift valuables off the floor

    Move rugs, boxes and electronics out of the splash zone. Keep power cords and outlets away from any standing water.

  3. 3

    Skip the chemical drain cleaner

    Liquid openers sitting in a blocked pipe can splash back on whoever works on it. Tell the dispatcher if you already poured some in.

  4. 4

    Call the 24/7 line

    Reach our dispatch and describe which fixtures are affected. After hours and overnight calls are handled the same way as daytime ones.

Kitchen sink clogs and how they get cleared

Kitchen drains clog differently from bathroom ones. Cooking grease cools inside the pipe, coats the walls, and catches coffee grounds, rice and scraps until the passage narrows to a straw. That is why a kitchen sink can go from slow to fully stopped in a single week.

We clear it with a cable that cuts through the buildup, then flush the line so the grease does not simply shift down to the next bend. If your dishwasher drains into the same line, we check that it runs clear too. Garbage disposal trouble is its own job, handled under Garbage Disposal Repair.

Shower and tub drains that will not empty

Standing water around your ankles in the shower is almost always hair wrapped in soap film just below the strainer. It forms a mat that holds water back. Pulling the strainer and cabling the trap usually fixes it without opening anything else.

If the tub and shower both drain slowly, the clog sits past the point where they join. Many Pico Rivera homes were built in the 1950s, and the census shows a median build year of 1957. Older drain lines tend to have rough interiors that catch debris, so a repeat clog in an older house is worth a closer look.

What shapes the price of a drain job

The quote you hear on the phone comes down to a few drain-specific things.

Where the clog sits

A fixture trap is quick work. A clog deep in a branch or the main line takes longer cable and more time.

Access to the line

A cleanout makes cabling straightforward. With no cleanout, we may need to go in through a fixture or a vent.

Type of blockage

Soft buildup clears fast. Roots, scale or a collapsed section need heavier tools or a separate repair.

Time of the call

A 3 a.m. visit is scheduled differently from a Tuesday one, and you hear the terms before we begin.

Follow-up work

A camera look or Hydro Jetting adds scope, and we describe it before doing it.

Snaking, cabling, and when it is more than a clog

Snaking and cabling are the same family of tools. A hand-fed snake handles a fixture trap. A powered cable on a drum reaches farther and spins a cutting head through grease, hair and light roots. Cabling the main line from a cleanout is the usual fix when several fixtures back up together.

When a line keeps clogging, a drain line leak is sometimes part of the story. A small crack lets soil and roots in and wet spots show up in a yard or crawlspace. A camera and a pressure check tell you whether you need cleaning or a repair, and our Leak Detection page explains how we confirm it.

For big grease or scale, the better answer may be Hydro Jetting, and for a main line that keeps failing, Sewer Line Cleaning covers the heavier work. If you live in Norwalk or Commerce, we run the same dispatch out to you, see our Norwalk page.
