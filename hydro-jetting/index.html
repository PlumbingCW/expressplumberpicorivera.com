Three jobs where jetting earns its place

These are the clogs where the extra step pays off.

Grease that keeps coming back

If a line clears for a few weeks and then slows again, the film on the wall is likely still there. Jetting removes it instead of punching through it.

Scale that narrows the bore

Mineral buildup grows slowly. Local tap water has an average hardness of 174.4 mg/L per the City of Pico Rivera's 2023 water report, about 10.2 grains per gallon, so buildup is a reasonable thing to check for.

Roots that need a thorough clean

Jetting can clear a root mass and the debris around it. Roots still return if the gap remains, so we look at the joint on camera.

When jetting is the wrong tool

Water pressure is not a repair. If the camera shows a cracked or offset pipe, a bellied section or a collapse, jetting can wash debris out for a day and leave the real problem in place. In those cases we move to repair options and explain why.

Old pipe is the other reason to be careful. Pressure is chosen to suit the pipe, and we set it conservatively on lines that look fragile. If we do not trust a line to take it, we will say so and use a gentler method.
