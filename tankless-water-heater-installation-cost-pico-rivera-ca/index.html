What Drives Tankless Water Heater Installation Cost in Pico Rivera

By Pico Rivera Express Plumbing · Updated October 23, 2026 · 6 min read

The price of a tankless install depends far more on your gas line, venting and wall layout than on the unit itself. This guide lists the factors that move the price, so you can read a quote with confidence instead of guessing.

Why two tankless quotes can be far apart

A tankless heater heats water as it flows, so it asks more of your gas line and your venting than a tank does. Two homes can get the same model and very different totals, because one needs a larger gas line and a new vent path and the other does not. We do not print dollar figures here, because any number would be wrong for your house.

What we can do is name every factor, so you can ask the right questions. Ask for each one in writing as a separate line. Call us if a quote does not show them and you want it explained.

The cost factors, one by one

Each item can move the total up or down.

Gas line capacityTankless units can burn a lot of gas at once. If the existing line is too small for the new demand, a larger line or a new run may be needed.
VentingTankless vents carry hot exhaust and need the right material and routing. A longer run, a new wall penetration or a roof path adds labor and parts.
Where the old tank satA closet or garage is easy to swap. Moving the heater to a new wall or outside means new gas, water and vent connections.
Water and gas connectionsNew shutoff and service valves, a gas shutoff and fittings are normal parts of the job. Corroded old connections add work.
Condensate and electricalMany units need a power outlet, and high-efficiency types need a condensate drain. Adding either takes time.
Permit and inspectionA permit step is part of the job in many cities. Ask who handles it and whether the fee is in the quote.
Unit size and featuresCapacity, recirculation features and brand all change the equipment price.
Old tank removal and patchingHauling the old tank, patching walls and cleaning up are separate lines on a quote.

Local note

Pico Rivera permit rules and hardness for a tankless swap

City criteria list an Express Permit for a tankless change out at the same location, open in single-family, estate, multiple-family, variable density, planned residential unit development and Specific Plan 301 zones. The contractor has to supply an active California State Contractor's License plus City business license details.

Two practical points follow for your quote. A swap in the same spot fits that permit, while moving the heater is a different question for Building and Safety. And a contractor who cannot show a licence should not be on your short list. City Hall at 6615 Passons Blvd keeps Monday to Thursday hours, 7:30 a.m. to 5:00 p.m., and permit rules vary.

On water, the city's 2023 report averages 174.4 mg/L of hardness, about 10.2 grains per gallon. Cal Water says hard water may build up minerals in water heaters, which is why the flush plan below matters for a tankless unit.

Keeping a tankless unit healthy after the install

Ask for these items to be written into your plan.

  1. 1

    Install service valves

    Isolation valves on the hot and cold lines let a plumber flush the heat exchanger without taking the unit apart.

  2. 2

    Set a flush schedule

    The manufacturer sets an interval for descaling. With hard water, shorter intervals are a sensible habit. Check the manual, then ask your plumber what fits.

  3. 3

    Clean the inlet screen

    A small screen at the cold inlet catches debris. Check it when you flush.

  4. 4

    Watch for error codes

    Codes tied to temperature or flow often mean scale or a blocked vent. Do not clear them again and again.

How to compare quotes without a price list

Ask each contractor to list what is included: the unit, gas line work, venting, valves, permit, haul-away and patching. A written quote with a total price and payment schedule is what the California contractor board recommends for home improvement work. Down payments should stay within the limit the state sets.

Our Tankless Water Heater page explains options, and Water Heater Installation covers the work itself. A walk-through lets us check gas and vent, then write a quote that names every line.

Be wary of a quote that is one number with no breakdown. If the gas line, vent and permit are missing, they will likely show up later as extras. Ask what happens if the plumber opens the wall and finds something unexpected, and get the answer in writing before work starts.

Safety checklist before any gas tankless work

Gas work is not a place for guessing.

  • ✓Smell gas or hear hissing near the heater and you leave the building first, then call SoCalGas at 1-800-427-2200 from a safe place.
  • ✓Shut off the gas and water to the old heater only if you can reach the valves without entering a gassy area.
  • ✓Never relight a pilot or open a vent on an appliance you suspect is leaking.
  • ✓Keep the area around the heater clear of boxes and chemicals.
  • ✓Ask the plumber to confirm gas line size, venting and permit needs in writing before work starts.
