Remodels and new construction

A remodel opens walls, and that is the cheapest time to fix the plumbing behind them. We look at existing supply and drain lines before the finish crew arrives, and tell you what we would replace while the wall is open.

On new construction or an addition, plumbing goes in before the walls close. We coordinate rough-in with your builder and set stub-outs where fixtures will land. For older homes with tired pipes, Repiping may belong in the same plan.

Gas smell? Leave first, then call

Smell gas or suspect a leak? Get everyone out, then phone SoCalGas on 1-800-427-2200 once you are clear of the building. Dial 911 as well if lives or property may be at risk, or if SoCalGas cannot be reached.

Once everyone is safe and the utility has cleared the area, we handle the non-emergency repair or install.

Gas line work, from a stub to a repair

We install and inspect gas lines for appliances, outdoor features and new equipment. Every job begins with a look at the existing line, the fittings and the shutoff, and we put what we find on the quote.

The City of Pico Rivera offers an Express Permit for a residential gas line repair. For a new gas line or a larger change, ask Building and Safety at (562) 942-2000, because permit rules vary.

What moves the price of an install

You see the price before we begin. These items change it.

Fixture countEach fixture adds supply, drain and shutoff work.
Distance and routingA long run through finished walls takes more labor than a short one in an open stud bay.
AccessSlab, crawlspace, attic and tight cabinets all change the time on site.
MaterialsFixture grade, valve type and pipe material are your choices and affect cost.
Permit and inspectionSome work carries a permit step, which we plan into the schedule.

Standards and sources behind this page
