What a plumbing failure does to a business besides the repair

A stopped sink or a leak is a continuity problem. These are the situations we hear described most.

Restaurants and food service

A backed-up floor drain or prep sink can stop service. Kitchens depend on sinks, drains and grease handling working together.

Retail shops

A leak from a restroom or ceiling can damage stock and displays. Closing one restroom may be enough, but only if the leak is contained.

Offices

A running toilet, a failed water heater or a ceiling drip can interrupt a workday. Office issues are often the easiest to schedule after hours.

Warehouses and shops

Floor drains, restroom blocks and water lines run long distances in larger buildings. Finding the right shutoff is the first job.

Commercial leak detection: what to expect

Finding a leak in a commercial building can take more than a visit. Access, shared walls and landlord rules decide how much we can check, and some leaks end up outside the tenant's space. Backflow testing in brief

A backflow preventer is a device on a water line that keeps water from flowing backward into the supply. Testing checks that it still does its job. Who must test and how often depends on your water provider and local requirements, so ask them what applies to your property.

Call us when you get a notice from your provider, after the device is repaired or when you see water coming out of it. We can discuss the test and what comes next, then coordinate with your provider on the paperwork.

Grease trap cleaning in brief

A grease trap catches fats, oils and grease from kitchen wastewater before it reaches the drain line. A full trap causes slow drains, odors and backups. Cleaning schedules depend on the size of the trap and the volume of cooking, and your landlord or local sanitation authority may have rules, so check with them.

Call when kitchen drains slow down, odors rise from the trap or the lid shows it is full. If a line has already backed up, our Drain Cleaning and Hydro Jetting pages cover how we clear the pipes behind the trap.
