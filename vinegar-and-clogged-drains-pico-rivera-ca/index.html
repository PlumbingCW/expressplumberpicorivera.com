Does Vinegar and Baking Soda Unclog a Drain? A Pico Rivera, CA Guide

By Pico Rivera Express Plumbing · Updated October 23, 2026 · 6 min read

Vinegar and baking soda can freshen a drain and loosen a light film, but they rarely clear a real clog. This guide explains what the fizz does, what it cannot touch and when to stop and call.

What the fizz can and cannot do

Baking soda and vinegar react to make carbon dioxide, water and a mild salt solution. That bubbling can loosen a thin layer of soap film or food residue near the drain opening. It does not break up hair mats, grease plugs, roots or a solid object.

So the mix is fine as light maintenance on a slow kitchen or bathroom sink. If water stands still, several fixtures back up, or the drain gurgles, skip the kitchen chemistry and call (562) 488-9614. Our dispatch runs 24/7.

A clog that returns within days usually means the cause is deeper than the fizz can reach.

If you still want to try it, do it this way

A careful attempt costs almost nothing. Keep expectations small and stop if water starts to rise.

  1. 1

    Remove standing water

    Bail out as much as you can. The mix needs contact with the drain, not a full basin to dilute it.

  2. 2

    Pour in baking soda, then vinegar

    Use about a half cup of baking soda followed by a half cup of white vinegar. Cover the drain with a plug or wet cloth for the first few minutes so the fizz works downward.

  3. 3

    Wait and flush with hot tap water

    Give it about 15 minutes, then run hot water from the tap. Plain tap hot water, not a boiling kettle, is the safer choice.

  4. 4

    Test, then stop

    If the water drains normally, you are done. If it does not, do not mix in chemical drain products on top of this, since leftovers can splash on whoever comes next.

Local note

Pico Rivera water and housing: what it means for you

Hardness is the local number that matters here. In its 2023 Consumer Confidence Report, the City of Pico Rivera lists an average total hardness of 174.4 mg/L, with a range from 121 to 212 mg/L. On the scale Cal Water uses in its own report, that average falls in the hard band, which runs 150 to 300 ppm.

Hard water leaves mineral scale on faucet aerators, showerheads and the inside of pipes. Vinegar does dissolve light scale on removable parts like aerators, so soaking those is a sensible use. It will not clear scale from inside a wall, and that is one more reason to move to a Drain Cleaning visit when a drain stays slow.

Pipe and fixture cautions

Stated carefully, since conditions vary from house to house.

Boiling water

Very hot or boiling water can stress a porcelain toilet or sink and may soften some plastic pipe or fittings. Use hot tap water as a default.

Old metal pipe

Acidic liquids sit longer in a stubborn drain and old metal fittings can already be thin. Do not leave vinegar in the line for hours.

Garbage disposals

The fizz is harmless to most disposals, but it will not clear a jam. A humming motor that will not turn is a repair, covered on our Garbage Disposal Repair page.

Unknown pipe material

If you are unsure what the line is made of, skip repeated treatments and ask for a camera look. Guessing is how a small clog becomes a bigger repair.

When to put the vinegar away and call

The home remedy has done all it can if you see any of these.

  • ✓Water backs up in more than one fixture.
  • ✓A clog returns within a week or two.
  • ✓The toilet gurgles when you run a sink.
  • ✓There is a sewage smell near a floor drain or cleanout.
  • ✓Water surfaces in a shower while the washer drains.
  • ✓You have already used the same drain several times this season.

Any of these points to something deeper than a surface film. We start with Drain Cleaning and move to hydro jetting when grease or scale is packed in. Book at (562) 488-9614.

What a plumber does that the pantry cannot

A cable or jetter removes the clog itself rather than hoping chemistry shifts it. We also look at why it formed, whether that is a low spot in the line, a bad connection or years of grease.

For a kitchen line packed with old grease, jetting scours the pipe wall instead of poking a hole through the middle. For a tub, a small cable usually pulls the hair mat out in one pass. Customers in Norwalk and Montebello often call after the third try with the fizz, and the answer is usually simple once the line is open.
