What decides the cost of a new water heater

Here is what sits behind the number, in the order it usually matters.

Tank size and typeA larger tank, a tankless unit or a heat pump model each carries a different equipment cost. Household size and peak use set the size.
Fuel and ventingReplacing gas with gas is the simplest path. Switching fuel adds gas line, electrical or venting work.
Location and accessA garage closet is straightforward. An attic, a tight alcove or a long haul from the truck adds hours.
Code upgrades on siteOlder installs often lack a drain pan, expansion control, proper strapping or a safe discharge line. We list what is missing before we start.
Permit handlingA same-location change-out may qualify for the city's Express Permit, described below. The permit step is part of the scope we quote.
TimingA night or weekend swap is priced as stated before we begin.

Tank failed overnight: the first hour

Water spreading from the base of the tank means the steel has given out. Close the cold supply valve, switch the heater off at the breaker or gas control, and open a hot tap in the house to relieve pressure. Mop what you can and move belongings aside.

If you smell natural gas, leave the house and call SoCalGas at 1-800-427-2200 from outside. Then call our 24/7 line. In many cases we can move on a same-day or after-hours replacement when available, and we will tell you plainly what the schedule looks like.

Tank, tankless or heat pump: the options to compare

If time allows, think about these. If the tank is flooding, we can swap like for like and revisit later.

Standard tank

The simplest swap. It fits the existing space and connections and keeps the visit short.

Tankless

Hot water on demand and a smaller footprint, with more involved gas or electrical needs. Read about Tankless Water Heater before deciding.

Heat pump (hybrid)

A different kind of unit with its own space and power needs. Ask us whether it suits your home. We do not make price or rebate claims here.

Larger or smaller tank

If the family has grown or shrunk since the last install, this is the time to resize.

Permits and the City of Pico Rivera Express Permit

The City of Pico Rivera lists an Express Permit for residential water heater change-outs in the same location, and also for tankless change-outs in the same location. The contractor must provide an active California State Contractor's License and City business license information.

That means the contractor, not you, supplies the licence details. Other permit rules vary, so ask the Pico Rivera Building and Safety division. City Hall is at 6615 Passons Blvd, open Monday to Thursday 7:30 a.m. to 5:00 p.m.

What happens on replacement day

A same-location swap follows a simple order.

  1. 1

    Isolate and drain

    We shut the water and fuel, then drain what is left in the old tank.

  2. 2

    Remove the old heater

    We disconnect, haul it out and check shutoffs, flex connectors and venting.

  3. 3

    Set and connect the new unit

    The tank is placed on a drain pan, strapped and connected, with a discharge line to a safe spot.

  4. 4

    Test and walk you through

    We refill, check for leaks, test gas or electrical, and show you the shutoffs. Older water pipes nearby may be noted for later, see Repiping.

Standards and sources behind this page
