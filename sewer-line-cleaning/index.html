How a main line gets cleaned, in the order it happens

This is the sequence on a typical sewer cleaning visit.

  1. 1

    Find the cleanout

    The cleanout is the capped pipe that gives direct access to the main line. We locate it, open it carefully, and check whether anything is already pushing back.

  2. 2

    Choose cable or water

    A cable cuts through soft clogs and roots. High-pressure water scours the whole pipe wall. Which one fits depends on what the line is holding.

  3. 3

    Clear the line

    We work from the cleanout toward the street connection and keep going until flow is steady.

  4. 4

    Camera the result

    A camera confirms the line is open and shows anything that will bring the clog back.

  5. 5

    Explain what we saw

    You get the footage, the cause, and a plan for keeping it from repeating.

Cabling versus hydro jetting

A cable, sometimes called a snake, is a rotating cutter on a flexible line. It is good at punching through a clog and cutting roots, and it is often the quicker and gentler first move. The limit is that it opens a channel and may leave buildup on the pipe wall.

Hydro jetting uses pressurized water to clean the full diameter of the pipe, which is why it suits grease and scale. It is not right for every pipe or every clog, and our Hydro Jetting page explains how we decide. We pick the method after looking at your line, not before.

Root intrusion

Roots go where water and tiny gaps are, and sewer joints qualify. A cable can remove a root mass, but it does not close the gap the roots came through. If the camera shows repeated root growth, we talk through ongoing maintenance and whether a repair on that section is worth doing.

If the pipe is also cracked, cleaning is a stopgap. Our Sewer Line Repair page covers what comes next.

Cleanout access: what to do before we arrive

A reachable cleanout is the difference between a quick start and a search.

  • ✓Find the cleanout cap, often a capped pipe near the house or along the side yard. If you cannot find it, tell dispatch and we will help you look.
  • ✓Clear a few feet around it of pots, boxes and plants.
  • ✓Keep pets inside and gate off the area.
  • ✓Note which drains are slow and which are fully blocked.
  • ✓Do not remove the cap if sewage is under pressure behind it. Wait for us.
  • ✓Turn off any irrigation that runs near the cleanout area.
