Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

This policy explains how Pico Rivera Express Plumbing handles information when you visit expressplumberpicorivera.com or call our phone line. We are located at 8600 Whittier Blvd, Pico Rivera, CA 90660.

Information we collect

This website has no forms, accounts or comment sections, so we do not collect information you type into the site.

When you call us, we may log the call, your phone number, your address and the details of your plumbing problem so we can dispatch a plumber, schedule work and keep service records.

Cookies and analytics

The site may load fonts from Google Fonts and an embedded map from Google Maps, which can set their own cookies under Google's policies. We do not run advertising trackers on these pages.

How we use information

  • To answer your call and send a plumber
  • To schedule, perform and follow up on work
  • To meet legal, tax and insurance requirements
  • To improve how we answer questions

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with the people needed to complete your service, such as the assigned plumber, or when the law requires it.

California privacy rights

California residents can ask what personal information we hold about them and request that we correct or delete it, subject to legal limits. Contact us at contact@expressplumberpicorivera.com or (562) 488-9614.

Changes

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do.

Coverage area

Where our plumbers dispatch from

Dispatch runs from Pico Rivera and reaches Montebello, Santa Fe Springs, Norwalk, Commerce and the neighborhoods between them.
