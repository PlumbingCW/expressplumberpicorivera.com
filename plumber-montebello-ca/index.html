Before we arrive: a burst pipe or active leak in a Montebello home

Do these four things while the crew is on the way.

Shut the main valve

Turn it clockwise until it stops. In many older Montebello homes it sits near the front hose bib or by the garage wall, so look there first.

Kill power near the water

If water is near outlets, a panel or appliances, switch off that breaker. Do not step into standing water to reach it.

Clear the area

Lift rugs, electronics and anything cardboard off the floor. Move children and pets to a dry room.

Call the 24/7 line

Ring (562) 488-9614 and tell dispatch where the water is coming from. They will pass the notes to the crew heading your way.
