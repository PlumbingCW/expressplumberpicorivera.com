The first ten minutes after a pipe bursts

Do these in order. Each one limits what the water can reach.

  1. 1

    Close the main water shutoff

    Find the main valve at the house, often near the meter or where the supply enters. Turn it fully closed. If it will not turn, tell the dispatcher right away.

  2. 2

    Kill power near the water

    If water is near outlets, appliances or the panel, switch off the breakers for that area. Never step into standing water to reach a panel.

  3. 3

    Open taps to drain the lines

    Open the lowest cold tap, and a hot tap as well, to bleed remaining water and drop pressure.

  4. 4

    Contain and clear

    Move rugs and electronics, lay towels along doorways, and use buckets under any active drip. Water can run along framing, so check the rooms below.

  5. 5

    Call the line

    Call dispatch and say where the water is, whether it is stopped, and what pipe material you can see.

A note on insurance, kept honest

Damage from a burst pipe is often the kind of loss that homeowner policies deal with, but we cannot tell you what yours covers. Read your policy or ask your insurer.

What kind of break is it?

The pipe material and the type of failure decide the fix.

What you seeLikely causeTypical repair
Spray from a copper linePinhole corrosion, a split or a failed jointCut out the section and sweat in new copper or use a fitting
Crack in a plastic or older steel lineAge, movement, pressure or frost-like stressReplace the cracked length
Seep at a joint or fittingFailed solder, loose connection or worn washerRework the joint or swap the fitting
Wet soil above a drain lineCracked or separated drain pipeLocate by camera, then replace the section

A single failure in an older pipe often means more weak spots. See Repiping if the rest of the line looks tired.

Cracked or broken pipe: what repair looks like

Once the water is off, we open only what we need to reach the break. We cut back to sound pipe, fit new material with the right connectors, and pressure-test before we close the wall. The goal is a repair that holds, not a patch.

Census figures show that 86.2 percent of Pico Rivera homes were built before 1980, and the median build year is 1957. In older houses, a burst is often the first visible sign that the pipe around it is aging. We note what we saw so you can decide whether to repair or replace more of the run.

Leaking pipe, drain pipe and copper pipe: how each is handled

A pinhole or slow weep is a leak, not a burst, but it can turn into one. We stop it with a cut-in section or a fitting and test the line afterward. If you cannot find the source, Leak Detection is the better start.

Drain pipe repair

A cracked drain sends wastewater into soil or crawlspace. We locate the break, replace the damaged length and check slope so flow stays smooth. Chronic clogs may also be involved, see Drain Cleaning.

Copper pipe repair

Copper fails at joints, at pinholes from corrosion, and at splits. We cut to clean pipe, fit new copper or an approved coupling, and test. If the rest of the copper line is thin, we say so.
