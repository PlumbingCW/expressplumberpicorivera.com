Toilet overflowing right now? Shut the angle stop first

Turn the angle stop clockwise until it stops. It is the small oval or round handle on the supply line behind the toilet, and closing it ends the refill that keeps feeding a bowl at the rim. If the handle is frozen or weeps when you turn it, close the main water valve for the house instead.

Do not flush again, and do not keep plunging a bowl that is near the top. Lift the tank lid and press the flapper down so the tank stops dumping into the bowl. Pull up rugs, then call our overnight line. We book same-day visits when available and take after-hours calls for floods that will not stop.

A toilet that only hisses or runs on can wait for a daytime visit. Sewage in the tub or several fixtures gurgling at once points to the line, not the bowl, so see Drain Cleaning and call right away.

Four things a failing toilet does and what each one usually means

The sound or the puddle tells us most of what we need before we open the tank.

It runs after the flush

Water keeps trickling into the bowl or the tank refills every few minutes. The flapper is the usual cause, followed by a fill valve that no longer shuts off.

It hisses all day

A hiss means the fill valve is leaking past its seat. This is a small part and a quick swap in most tanks.

Water at the base

A damp ring around the bowl points to the wax seal or the closet bolts. Stop using the toilet, because a failed seal soaks the subfloor over time.

The bowl rocks

A bowl that shifts under you has loose bolts or a damaged flange. Rocking breaks the seal, so this one is worth fixing promptly.

Run the food coloring test before you buy a single part

The EPA suggests a simple check for a silent tank leak. Drop a little food coloring into the tank, wait 10 minutes without flushing, and look at the bowl. If color shows up there, water is slipping past the flapper.

The EPA also advises replacing a toilet flapper at least every five years. That is a cheap part that tends to be forgotten, so ask us to look at it on any toilet visit. A quiet tank leak never shows on the floor, but it shows on the bill. If your bill jumped and you cannot find a cause, our Leak Detection page explains the next step.

What a 1957 median home means for your toilet

The Census ACS 2024 estimates put the median home in Pico Rivera at 1957, and 86.2 percent of the 17,352 units were built before 1980. In houses that old, the toilet may be a replacement from a past remodel or the original fixture. Ask us the age of the one you have, because an original bowl with a cracked tank is a replace call, not a repair call.

While you wait for the technician

Five minutes of prep saves time on the visit.

  • ✓Close the angle stop behind the bowl. If it will not turn, tell us when you call the dispatch line.
  • ✓Take a photo of the leak area and the tank interior before you touch anything.
  • ✓Clear the space around the toilet so the technician can reach the bolts and supply line.
  • ✓Move towels and bath mats out of the wet zone, and keep power strips off the floor.
  • ✓Note when the problem began and whether other drains in the house are slow.

If the toilet is clogged and nothing else is slow, tell us that too. A single-fixture clog is a faster fix than a line problem, and see Plumbing Installation if you want to upgrade the fixture, and Faucet Repair if a sink nearby is dripping too.
