Confirm the leak before you pay to find it

The EPA suggests a simple meter check. It costs nothing and tells you whether to schedule a visit.

  1. 1

    Stop every water use

    Turn off taps, the dishwasher, washer and ice maker. Make sure no one flushes a toilet during the test.

  2. 2

    Read the meter

    Note the dial or digital reading, including the small leak indicator if there is one.

  3. 3

    Wait two hours

    Do not use any water in that time.

  4. 4

    Read it again

    EPA says that if the meter changes at all, you probably have a leak. Test toilets next with a few drops of food coloring in the tank. Color in the bowl after 10 minutes, without flushing, means a flapper leak.

What a running meter or high bill is telling you

EPA says average household leaks waste more than 9,300 gallons a year, and about nine percent of homes leak 50 gallons or more a day. A family of four using over 12,000 gallons a month in a cooler month may have a serious leak.

If your bill jumped and the meter test shows movement, the water is going somewhere. Your water provider may be the City of Pico Rivera Water Division or Pico Water District, so check which one bills you before you ask a billing question. Call us to book the location work.

Where the leak is hiding: location by location

Each location has its own clue and its own way in.

Ceiling or wallA brown stain, bubbling paint or a soft spot. We map the wet area, then trace the supply or drain line above it. Turn off the water if the stain is growing.
Under the house or crawlspaceDamp soil, a musty smell, or a floor that bounces. Pipes in a crawlspace are often visible once we get in, so detection can be fast.
Underground or buried lineA meter that runs when everything is off, a wet patch in a dry yard, or low pressure. Listening and pressure tests narrow the dig. See Water Line Repair for the fix.
SlabWarm floor spots or a hum from the floor. Covered in depth under Slab Leak Repair.
Irrigation or sprinklerSoggy turf or a zone that runs weak. EPA notes a 1/32 inch irrigation leak can waste about 6,300 gallons a month, so shut the zone and call.
Yard and meter boxWater filling the meter box or pooling near it usually means a leak on your side. Check the valve and fittings, and call us if the meter still moves.
Pool or spaA pool losing more than normal evaporation may have a plumbing leak. We test the supply and return lines, but pool shell leaks need a pool specialist.

Active leak or flooding: when the clock matters

Water spreading through a ceiling, a wet outlet, or a meter that spins with everything off is urgent. Close the main valve at the house, turn off power to wet areas at the breaker, and move belongings aside. Take photos of damage before you clean up.

We treat these as same-day and after-hours calls. Many Pico Rivera homes are older, with 86.2 percent built before 1980 per the census, so aging pipe is a common source and worth testing. Ask about Burst Pipe Repair if a line has already failed.
